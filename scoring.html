Scoring and interpretation

Two separate questions: how much harm could occur, and how well are safeguards in place?

HomeInstructionsQuick assessmentThorough assessmentScoringPolicy & data
Read two results side by side. Risk indicates how serious the system’s potential effects may be. Practice indicators show whether safeguards are evidenced or need improvement. Strong safeguards do not make a high impact system low risk.

1. Quick assessment route

Quick uses four short prompts. It returns the highest concern indicated; answers are never averaged. A credible severe harm or potentially prohibited use concern turns the route red and calls for specialist review.

RouteTypical signalSuggested next step
Low · greenSmall, bounded use; little direct influence on people; no sensitive data or serious unresolved concern.Complete the Quick safeguards. Close the review only when evidence and residual risk support it.
Medium · amberPersonal data, wider reach, some uncertainty, or a meaningful but manageable effect.Continue to Thorough and define safeguards, owners and review points.
High · orangePotentially significant effect on education opportunities, services, resources, rights, safety, employment or reputation; sensitive data; large scale; or limited human review.Complete Thorough and obtain strong governance or specialist oversight.
Pause · redA credible concern about prohibited use or severe, hard to reverse harm.Pause routine progression and seek local legal, rights, safety and ethics review.

If a prompt is unclear, use the more cautious route and investigate. A route is a starting point for human review, not a legal finding.

2. Thorough assessment risk screen

Five questions contribute points. The sixth checks for possible unlawful use or severe harm and can override the score. The result is a screening signal for discussion, not an automatic decision.

Low · green

Below 25%, with no high concern answer or uncertainty. Complete the six principle sections and record the final decision.

Medium · amber

25% to 49%, one high concern answer, or any “Unsure” answer. Define safeguards, owners and review points.

High · orange

50% or more, two high concern answers, or sensitive information combined with a serious effect. Seek strong governance or specialist review.

Pause · red

“Yes” to possible unlawful use or severe harm overrides the percentage. Pause and seek specialist review.

The five scored questions have a maximum total of 15 points. The total is divided by 15 and rounded to a whole percentage. One high concern answer raises the route to at least Medium; two raise it to High.

3. Six principle indicators

The 15 practice questions are grouped under the six principles on the home page. “Yes, with evidence” scores 2, “Partly or in progress” scores 1, and both “No” and “Information not yet available” score 0. “Not applicable, with a reason” is excluded.

Equal principle weightEach principle percentage is calculated from its applicable questions. The overall practice indicator is the simple average of the six percentages, so sections with more questions do not dominate.

IndicatorMeaningAction
Green · In placeThe safeguard is operating and the team has evidence.Keep evidence current and set a review trigger.
Amber · StrengthenWork is partial, planned, or the answer generated a follow up prompt.Define the missing work, owner, due date and completion evidence.
Red · GapA safeguard is absent or a critical concern remains.Prioritise corrective action. Pause where serious harm could occur.
Grey · Not applicableThe team considers an item irrelevant to this context.Record a short reason. Do not use this to hide missing evidence.

Quick counts statuses across eight core checks. Thorough reports six principle percentages and answer level follow up prompts. Practice status is never added to the risk screen percentage.

4. Residual risk after safeguards

The final three prompts record the priority risks, the actions needed and the team’s decision. When the team records a remaining risk level, keep separate risks separate and do not average away a serious one.

Residual levelColorInterpretation and response
LowGreenLimited effects remain plausible, controls are evidenced and little uncertainty remains. Keep routine monitoring.
MediumAmberMeaningful effects remain possible or safeguards need strengthening. Assign actions, owners, dates and monitoring.
HighRedSerious effects remain plausible, controls are inadequate or uncertainty is material. Revise or pause for an authorised decision.

Explain each rating using severity, likelihood, who may be affected, evidence and uncertainty. Record whether the overall residual risk is acceptable, conditional or too high to proceed.

Use the result to make a decision

Read the risk routeConfirm why the screen suggested that category. Add evidence and note uncertainty.
Find the practice gapsUse amber and red safeguards and their follow up prompts to identify work that is missing.
Assign action and reviewName an owner, due date, evidence of completion and a review trigger. Escalate residual High or unresolved concerns.

These are working rules for an international education self assessment. Apply the relevant national or local laws, education policies, institutional responsibilities and authorised decision processes.
