Before you begin

Work through this review as a team

Bring together the functions needed for this use, such as education policy, operations, data, technology, procurement, legal, privacy, security, accessibility and professional practice. Include representatives of affected groups when appropriate.

About this form

This form supports structured review of AI used in teaching, administration, planning, inspection, assessment, research, learner services and other education functions.

Prepare for local use

Interpret the questions using the laws, institutional responsibilities, education policies, languages, infrastructure and social conditions of the relevant country or jurisdiction.

The categories remain consistent across contexts. Evidence and examples should reflect the education setting, system level and people affected by the use.

Start with the education context

Describe the use, the level of the education system and who may be affected.
