Before you begin

Work through this review as a team

Include people who understand the education purpose, affected groups, data, technology, operations, procurement and applicable rules. This may involve education authorities, institutions, technical teams, school leaders, inspectors, educators, students or families, depending on the use.

About this form

Use this concise self assessment for an early review or a bounded use of AI anywhere in an education system. Four risk prompts suggest a Low, Medium or High route.

When to go further

Continue to Thorough when the use may influence learning opportunities, assessment, access, resource allocation, inspection, employment or another important education process.

The result follows the highest concern selected, not an average. Interpret each answer within the applicable national or local education, legal and institutional context.

Core safeguards

What you getSafeguard status and a reason for any item marked not applicable.
