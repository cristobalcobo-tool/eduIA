What this is

A common structure for reviewing AI across diverse education systems

AI can influence teaching, administration, planning, assessment, inspection, allocation of resources, employment and services for learners and families. This assessment helps teams examine the purpose, evidence, safeguards and consequences of a specific use.

A team self assessment

Use the functions relevant to the case, including education policy, operations, data, technology, procurement, legal, privacy, security, accessibility and professional practice. Include affected groups where appropriate. The result reflects the team’s evidence and judgement.

01 · CONTEXT

Education system AI

For authorities, institutions and teams that commission, design, procure, operate or oversee AI inside and outside the classroom.

02 · GOAL

Find risks early

Identify possible harm, examine safeguards and make uncertainty visible before procurement, pilot, deployment or expansion.

03 · PRACTICAL VALUE

Turn findings into action

Record what is working, what needs improvement, who is responsible and when the team will review it.

Responsible AI principles

Common categories, interpreted for education

Apply the same categories at system, authority, institution, programme and classroom levels, using evidence relevant to the country and use.

Human rights, dignity and equity

Examine unequal effects across learners, staff, institutions, languages, locations and social groups.

Transparency and explainability

Make AI use, responsibility and important results understandable and open to review.

Security and robustness

Test the system in representative education settings and prepare for failure, misuse and incidents.

Privacy and data protection

Limit and protect learner, staff and other personal data throughout its lifecycle.

Human oversight and accountability

Give responsible people the authority, information and time to intervene, explain and correct.

Sustainability and wellbeing

Consider wellbeing, professional roles, costs, infrastructure, supplier dependency and environmental effects.

Risk route at a glance

Use impact to choose the level of review

Interpret the route in the applicable education, legal and institutional context.

Low

Limited foreseeable impact and a bounded use. Begin with Quick and record the basis.

Medium

Meaningful interaction, personal data, wider reach or uncertainty. Use Thorough when safeguards need specific review.

High

Potentially significant effects on opportunities, services, resources, rights, safety or reputation. Use Thorough and strong governance review.

Excessive or unresolved

Potentially unlawful or severe harm. Pause routine progression and seek appropriate specialist review.

The team records its reasoning. A risk category is separate from the later rating of residual risk after safeguards.
